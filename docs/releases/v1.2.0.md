Charlie 1.2.0 adds **Earnings Calls** to Equity Research and the Events Calendar. Add your free Equibles API key in **Settings → API Connections**, open a company in **Equity**, and choose **Earnings Calls**. You can also open a transcript directly from a matching calendar event.

- **Call Brief** brings together the executive summary, bullish and bearish points, management guidance, and notable quotes.
- **Transcript** lets you search by word or speaker and compare spoken financial figures with corrections from official filings.
- **Analyst Q&A** identifies the analyst, firm, and responding executives. Open an answer to read the original exchange or jump to it in the transcript.
- **Trends** shows which topics appeared, persisted, or dropped out. Search for a word or phrase across the latest 4, 8, or 12 calls.

Coverage varies by company and quarter. Charlie caches results and loads additional material on demand to conserve the free plan’s daily allowance. [Equibles API limits](https://equibles.com/docs/rate-limits)

### Downloads

- **Apple Silicon Macs** can use the DMG installer or the ZIP app bundle. The Mac build is unnotarized.
- **Windows** users can choose Setup or Portable for x64 or ARM64.
- **Linux** users can [run Charlie from source](https://github.com/alcardaci/Charlie/tree/v1.2.0#install-and-run).
