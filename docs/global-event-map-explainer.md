# The News workspace

The Global Event Map and Country Hotspots share one card. Select a country on the map to jump to its events and original reporting. Choose a lookback from one hour to seven days. The source line shows how many articles each feed returned in the selected window.

Yahoo Finance and GDELT load independently. Charlie searches GDELT DOC and uses the public Global Knowledge Graph archive when the search is slow, rate-limited or unavailable. The archive supplies article titles, publisher URLs and GDELT indexing times from recent quarter-hour batches. A longer lookback can include earlier batches already collected during the session. Archive mode samples available batches. It does not retrieve every article from the full lookback period. [GDELT DOC](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/) · [GDELT article titles](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gkg-2-0-now-includes-page-titles/)

Charlie automatically sends new headlines to the OpenAI connection configured in Settings. The map shows only events that OpenAI identifies, locates and scores. Duplicate reports of one incident count once. Each country’s impact estimate combines the model’s scores for its incidents. If a review fails, the map can still show recent AI assessments for the same reports and marks the review as incomplete. The News feed and the published Caldara–Iacoviello geopolitical risk index are available independently of OpenAI.

The GEO topic in the News feed lists articles from both sources from the past 24 hours. Successful feed snapshots can be restored after reopening the app while their articles are still inside the selected window.

Each country shows its summary first, followed by its estimated impact and publisher links. The News feed carries the headlines. In **Edit layout**, drag a card onto the center of another to swap positions. Drop it along an edge to place it beside, above or below the target. An outside edge spans the workspace. Drag a corner or a divider to resize. On a narrow screen the cards stack and their heights can be adjusted individually. **Save layout** restores the arrangement on the next launch. Leaving News returns the same player to the bottom right and preserves the selected channel.
